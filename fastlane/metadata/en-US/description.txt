Avialobik is a dark, full-screen house desk for one night on the floor. It splits an opening cash amount across named doors, rings walk-ins once every door is seated, and runs an eight-name house card. It is for the person working that night: seating cash, counting doors, and advancing the on-deck pair.

• Intro pages "Cash", "Place", and "Ring", with "Next", "Continue", and "Skip".
• Four doors — "North door", "East door", "West door", and "Box office" — with caps of $50.00, $40.00, $30.00, and $20.00. Opening cash is $140.00 under "Cash".
• A draggable "Remaining lot" chip, and "Seat cash" on the selected door. Door lines read "Health … Cash …" and "Health … Cap …". "Health" is a door score.
• "Ring walk-in" posts an $8.00 walk-in once every door is seated. "Comp member" opens "Comp", where "Comp admission" posts a walk-in that adds $0.00 for a "Member number" of 8 to 14 digits.
• "Clock" shows the roster and an open shift, with "Add name" and "Clock in". Starter names are "Ada Quill" ("Treasurer"), "Moss Keene" ("Door"), and "Ivo Brant" ("House").
• "Bracket" shows the "House card" with "Nim", "Pell", "Sora", "Vetch", "Quill", "Hart", "Bram", and "Lark". "Seed pairings" builds the pairs. "Advance on deck" and on-deck name buttons post the winner.
• "Finance" marks "Count this door" or "Counted", then "Post the count". Totals are "Opening", "Seated", "Walk-ins", and "Day".
• "Settings", headed "Desk", has "Opening cash", "Save cash", "Log maint", "Show intro again", "Reset all data", and "Contact Avialobik".
• "Goals" shows "Still to place" and seats cash until the bottom button reads "Night target met".
• The same night returns on the next launch until "Reset all data". A Watch face shows "Doors" and "Seat the open cash on the door." It does not edit the night.

Amounts are US dollars with two decimal places. The interface is dark. There is no sign-in. Minimum iOS version is 17.0.

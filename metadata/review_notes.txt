Avialobik requests no permissions. No usage-description string is shown, because none is configured.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content shared with other people, no account-deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Names, member numbers, and the night stay in the app. "Reset all data" clears that night. It is not an account deletion.

Avialobik is a dark, full-screen house desk for one night on the floor. It splits an opening cash amount across named doors, rings walk-ins once every door is seated, and runs an eight-name house card. It is for the person working that night: seating cash, counting doors, and advancing the on-deck pair. No credentials or demo account are needed.

Cold launch opens a three-page intro. There is no tab bar and no sign-in. Page one is titled "Cash", with the line "Split tonight's opening cash across the doors before anyone rings in." and the buttons "Next" and "Skip". "Next" opens "Place", with "Drag the remaining cash onto the open door. Every door has to match first." "Next" again opens "Ring", with "After the cash matches, ring walk-ins and advance the card." and the buttons "Continue" and "Skip". "Skip" on any page, or "Continue" on the last page, leaves the intro the same way. The floor then shows the large title "Open" and the line "Cash is still open. Seat the remaining amount on the last door." The cash card is titled "Cash" and reads "Opening $140.00. Walk-ins $0.00." The board lists "North door", "East door", "West door", and "Box office". The chip's accessibility name is "Remaining lot", labeled "$140.00". Tap a door, then "Seat cash", or drag "Remaining lot" onto a door that still has room. A successful seat reads "Cash seated. Left …". When the remainder is $0.00, "Seat cash" is dimmed. The floor title can then read "Closed", with "Every door is seated. Ring a walk-in, or seed the card." "Ring walk-in" before that reads "Ring refused. Seat every door first." After every door is seated, "Ring walk-in" reads "Walk-in posted. Taken …". "Comp member" opens the sheet "Comp". The copy is "Type the member number. A comp posts a walk-in with no cash added." Enter 8 to 14 digits in "Member number" and tap "Comp admission". Fewer or more digits leaves the sheet open and the floor reads "Plate refused. Use 8 to 14 digits." "Clock" is headed "Open". "Roster" lists "Ada Quill" with "Treasurer", "Moss Keene" with "Door", and "Ivo Brant" with "House". "Clock in" is dimmed because the shift is already open. Type in "Name" and tap "Add name" to add that name with the role "Door". A blank name does nothing. "Bracket" is headed "House card". Tap "Seed pairings" only after every door holds its opening cash. Success reads "Pairings live. Tap the on-deck name." The floor title becomes "Live", with "Card live. Ring doors. Advance the on-deck pair." Tap a button whose title ends in ", on deck", or tap "Advance on deck". Success reads "Advance posted." "Finance" shows "Still open", "Count this door", and "Post the count". "Settings" is headed "Desk". Before any cash is seated, "Opening cash" (placeholder "Cents", starter value 14000) and "Save cash" can store a whole cent amount greater than zero. "Log maint" raises that door's "Health" number by one. "Show intro again" plays the three pages and returns to the same night. "Reset all data" asks "Reset all data? Stations, lots, and the tape are removed from this device." with "Reset all data" and "Cancel". "Goals" is a separate sheet and is not one of the floor buttons "Clock", "Bracket", "Finance", or "Settings". If it is open, it is titled "Goals", labeled "Still to place", and its bottom button starts as "Seat cash on North door" and later reads "Night target met". On Apple Watch, the face shows "Doors" and "Seat the open cash on the door." It does not edit the night.

The night stays on this device. "Contact Avialobik" in "Settings" opens the support page.
